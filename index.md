## Favorite CPSC 120L Labs

* Lab 8 part 2 :

I had an amazing time working in this lab. It involved using loops to make a pattern that extended up to 22 lines. Each line had a total of 22 bar and dash characters, with the little star symbol(*) in between the, behind it was a new line and kept the pattern going, At the end it was not too challenging, but it was pretty enjoyable. 

* Lab 11 part 2:

This lab seemed similar to lab 4 part 2, but with more TODO and more advanced. The main difference was that in lab 04, the guesser had only two guesses to guess the secret number, and in lab 11, we had to increase the guesses to four. We had to use boolean functions with if statements to check if the guess was right or wrong. This lab was actually the most fun I had in coding, and it was most definitely more challenging than lab 04.

* Lab 12:

This lab had to be the coolest lab i had to work on, it had a combination of RGB and RNG to make images in a cool way, i'm not gonna lie it was a bit difficult to code at the beginning, but as i went, i understood it and the final result was absolutely worth it. One of the things I learned to do was how to use RNG to adjust the amount of color I wanted, this lab was the coolest one by far.
